Chapter: Inventory & WIP Locator Management
Introduction
While ERP manages global inventory, MES tracks Shop Floor Inventory and WIP at a granular level. The Inventory module defines physical or logical locations where materials and containers are stored.

Classes:
- Warehouse: A high-level storage facility on the shop floor.
- Locator: A specific bin, shelf, or zone within a warehouse.
- InventoryBalance: The real-time record of what materials and quantities reside in a locator.
