Chapter: Electronic Signatures (21 CFR Part 11)
Introduction
For medical devices, pharma, and aerospace, operators must digitally sign certain actions (like scrap, rework, or parameter changes) to ensure FDA compliance.

Classes:
- SignatureRule: Defines what transactions require an E-Signature, and how many approvers are needed.
- SignatureRole: The specific authority level required (e.g., QA Manager, Lead Operator).
- SignatureLog: The immutable audit record of a captured signature.
