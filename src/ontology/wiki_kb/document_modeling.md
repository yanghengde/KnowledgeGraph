Chapter: Document Control
Introduction
Document Control is essential in MES to ensure that operators always have access to the most up-to-date Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), CAD drawings, and safety instructions. Camstar attaches Document models directly to Specs, Products, or Tasks.

Classes:
- Document: The metadata representing a physical or digital file (e.g., SOP-001).
- DocumentRevision: The versioned instance of a document (e.g., Rev A, Rev B). Only the active revision is displayed to the operator.
- DocumentGroup: A logical collection of related documents.
