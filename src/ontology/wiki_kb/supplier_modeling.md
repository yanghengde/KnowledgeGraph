Chapter: Supplier and Vendor Management
Introduction
The Supplier module tracks the origin of raw materials. Tracing a defect back to a specific vendor's batch is critical in modern manufacturing.

Classes:
- Vendor: The third-party company supplying the materials.
- VendorMaterial: The mapping between the supplier's part number and the internal Material number.
- ReceivingLog: A transaction record detailing when a vendor batch was received into the warehouse.
