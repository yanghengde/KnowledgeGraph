Chapter : Regulatory Agency Modeling
Introduction
The RegulatoryAgency entity (CdoId: 7883/7885) defines regulatory agencies
(e.g., FDA, EMA, NMPA) for compliance reporting with submission period configuration.

Field Definitions:
- Name (String, Required): Agency name.
- SubmissionPeriod (Integer, Required): Submission period in days.
- Plus standard fields: Description, Notes, FilterTags, IsFrozen, InstanceLocked, ChangeHistory, IconId.
