Chapter: Scrap and Cost Tracking
Introduction
The Scrap module tracks financial and material losses on the shop floor. When products are destroyed, scrapped, or lost, it records the reason and attributes the cost to a specific department or cost center.

Classes:
- ScrapReason: The categorized reason for scrapping a unit (e.g., Dropped, Burned, Machine Fault).
- CostCenter: The financial bucket responsible for taking the loss.
- ScrapLog: The transaction record containing the quantity lost and the financial impact.
