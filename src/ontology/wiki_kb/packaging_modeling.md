Chapter: Packaging Management
Introduction
The Packaging module manages the hierarchy and packing rules for finished goods. It defines how Containers (individual units or batches) are packed into Boxes, and Boxes onto Pallets for shipment.

Classes:
- Box: A packaging unit that holds multiple containers.
- Pallet: A shipping unit that holds multiple boxes.
- PackingRule: Defines how many units go into a box, and how many boxes go onto a pallet.
