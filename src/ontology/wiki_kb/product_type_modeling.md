Chapter : Product Type Modeling
Introduction
The ProductType entity (CdoId: 4290) defines product classification types used in product
and material management (e.g., finished goods, semi-finished, raw materials, packaging).

In This Chapter
- ProductType (Product Type)

Field Definitions:
- Name (String, Required): Unique product type name.
- WipMsgDefMgr (Navigation): WIP message definition manager.
- Plus standard fields: Description, Notes, FilterTags, IsFrozen, InstanceLocked, ChangeHistory, IconId.
